Day 1 — Points

UNDAS @ Quezon City

The Philippines celebrates multiple holidays throughout the year. Among these holidays are the first two days of November which are commonly referred to as UNDAS. During this time, Filipinos would traditionally visit their departed loved ones, bringing them flowers and offering prayers. So... for the first day of this challenge, I mapped several resting places I found through Google Maps, focusing on my home city — Quezon City.

Day 6-8 — Dimensions, Accesibility, Urban

Libreng Sakay sa QCity Bus Service

The Quezon City Government has a program called "Libreng Sakay sa QCity Bus Service" which, as the name suggests, offers free bus rides across 8 routes. I have only tried one bus route (Route #2) so I made a little simulation of its first hour of operation where the buses leave in 6 minutes interval during weekdays based on the QC website (see link below). Note: A uniform speed of 20kph was used here + 5 minutes loading/unloading time.
